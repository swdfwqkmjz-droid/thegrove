## Hardware

### Guitars

#### Taylor Jewel Kilcher Signature Model

Color: Surf Green  
Modifications:  
- TUSQ string tree for improved tuning stability and string movement.  
- Brass bridge saddles for enhanced sustain and tone.  

This 27" scale baritone guitar provides rich, deep tones perfect for ambient music and experimental styles.  

---

#### Harley Benton JA-60CC

Color: Seafoam Green  
Modifications:  
- Replacement roller bridge for smoother tremolo use and better intonation.  
- TUSQ string tree for improved tuning performance.  

A budget-friendly offset guitar inspired by classic designs, ideal for shimmering chords and melodic lines. Though it's a 25" scale, I keep it in baritone tuning, B to B.

### Audio Interfaces
#### Arturia MiniFuse 4 Audio Interface

Features:  
- 4-in/4-out audio interface, up to 24-bit/192kHz.  
- Includes MIDI I/O for seamless integration with other gear.  

Compact and versatile, perfect for recording both guitars and synths.

---

### MIDI Controllers
#### Arturia MiniLab MkI MIDI Keyboard

Features:  
- 25 velocity-sensitive keys.  
- 16 assignable rotary knobs for controlling synth parameters and effects.  
- Two touch strips for pitch bend and modulation.    

The original MiniLab, offering solid build quality and essential MIDI functionality. Perfect for portable music production and live improvisation.

#### Steam Deck (Running Generative MIDI & Sound Engines)

Software:
- [Midinous](https://nornec.itch.io/midinous): A grid-based generative MIDI sequencer, perfect for creating randomized, loop-based ambient compositions.
- [Carla](https://kx.studio/Applications:Carla): A versatile plugin host and patchbay, used to route MIDI and audio between software tools.
- [Decent Sampler](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/): A lightweight sampler hosting instruments like:
  - Electric Bell Toy Piano
  - Santa Monica Waves
  - Reel to Reel Piano
- [Dragonfly Reverb](https://michaelwillis.github.io/dragonfly-reverb/): Open-source reverb plugin used to add rich ambiance and depth.

The Steam Deck serves as a powerful, portable tool for generative ambient music. Using Midinous, I create random and loop-based MIDI patches, routed to multiple instances of Decent Sampler for unique textures and instrument layers.

## Apps & Effects

### Mixers
#### AUM

Purpose: Modular audio mixer, recorder, and router.  

Central to my DAWless workflow, AUM provides flexible audio routing, MIDI control, and seamless app integration. Perfect for creating and managing live performances or complex studio setups.  

[AUM](https://apps.apple.com/app/aum-audio-mixer/id1055636344)

---

#### Audiobus

Purpose: Audio and MIDI routing app.  

A classic app for chaining multiple apps together, enabling creative workflows by connecting instruments, effects, and recording tools.  

[Audiobus](https://apps.apple.com/app/audiobus/id558513570)

---

### Noise, Loops, & Effects
#### Gauss

Purpose: Looper with tape-like characteristics.  

Offers vintage-style looping with adjustable tape effects, allowing for organic, evolving textures in ambient music.  

[Gauss](https://apps.apple.com/app/gauss-field-looper/id1507325713)

---

#### iDensity

Purpose: Granular synthesis app.  

Perfect for creating rich soundscapes by manipulating and layering sound grains in real time.  

[iDensity](https://apps.apple.com/app/idensity/id432380315)

---

#### Weeping Wall

Purpose: Filter effect inspired by resonant wall filters.  

Adds deep, resonant, and rhythmic filtering effects, ideal for textural experimentation.  

[Weeping Wall](https://apps.apple.com/app/weeping-wall/id1525296906)

---

#### Ridgewalk

Purpose: Atmospheric texture generator.  

Designed to create evolving, noise-based soundscapes and textures.  

[Ridgewalk](https://apps.apple.com/app/ridgewalk/id1552872701)

---

#### Reels - LoFi Tape

Purpose: Tape emulation effect.  

Simulates the warmth, saturation, and imperfections of cassette tape, perfect for LoFi aesthetics.  

[Reels](https://apps.apple.com/app/reels/id1479229178)

---

#### Wires - Vintage Wire Echoes

Purpose: Echo and reverb effect.  

Inspired by vintage military wire recorders, Wires creates haunting, otherworldly echoes.  

[Wires](https://apps.apple.com/app/wires/id1512060317)

---

#### FieldScaper

Purpose: Field recording and sound manipulation tool.  

Transforms field recordings into ambient textures and experimental sounds.  

[Fieldscaper](https://apps.apple.com/app/fieldscaper/id931908514)

---

#### WoodLofier

Purpose: LoFi effect processor.  

Adds warmth, saturation, and other LoFi characteristics to audio signals.  

[WoodLofier](https://apps.apple.com/app/woodlofier/id1507721528)

---

#### Pitch Drift - Baby Audio

Purpose: Pitch modulation effect.  

Adds subtle pitch drift and detune effects for a more organic and vintage feel.  

[Pitch Drift](https://apps.apple.com/app/pitch-drift/id1643236049)
