Semi-nerdy but casual enough for a beach read if you're interested in the topic—WHICH I AM. Chock full of details about how on earth Brunelleschi managed to build a dome without any scaffolding, and all the reality show-level drama of the political and social scene around the effort. The 1-star off is only because it would have benefitted from some "The Way Things Work"-style original illustrations to make some of the building methods a bit clearer.
