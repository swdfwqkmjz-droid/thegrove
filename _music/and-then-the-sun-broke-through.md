Originally created as an entry for Starbucks' Off the Clock project, this piece was made entirely from acoustic guitar sounds chopped up and run through various plugins and the like. I'm still especially happy with some of the manual stutter edits at various points in the piece. It's meant to evoke a feeling of hope that follows uncertainty. The original mix was an amateur mixing disaster, and Starbucks was correct to pass on it. I'm happier with this one where I brought back a lot of the low end that I'd originally EQ'd out.
